Table salt dissolves in water as \ce{NaCl -> Na+ + Cl-}.

The combustion of hydrogen, \ce{2H2 + O2 -> 2H2O}, releases \pu{572 kJ}.

The Haber process reaches equilibrium: \ce{N2 + 3H2 <=> 2NH3}.

Sulfate precipitates with barium: \ce{SO4^2- + Ba^2+ -> BaSO4 v}.

Carbonate decomposes on heating: \ce{CaCO3 -> CaO + CO2 ^}.
