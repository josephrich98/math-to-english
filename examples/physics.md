Newton's second law states \(\vec{F} = m\vec{a}\).

The kinetic energy is \(E = \frac{1}{2} m v^2\).

Acceleration due to gravity is about \(\SI{9.8}{m/s^2}\).

The gradient of a scalar field is \(\grad{\phi}\), and Gauss's law relates
\(\div{\vec{E}}\) to charge density.

In quantum mechanics the commutator \(\comm{x}{p} = i\hbar\) is fundamental,
and \(\braket{\phi}{\psi}\) is the inner product of two states.
